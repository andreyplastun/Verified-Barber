---
name: Photo traffic optimization boundaries
description: Why photo savings must not imply mass production processing or original deletion.
---

Optimize new uploads and browser loading without rewriting legacy URLs or deleting original storage objects on replacement. Treat legacy backfill as a separate opt-in operation.

**Why:** The approved scope explicitly preserves existing links and full-size quality and prohibits unapproved production processing. Image inefficiency was confirmed, but its share of Supabase egress was not measured; fixture compression ratios must not be presented as total production savings. Paid Supabase Image Transformations are not an acceptable dependency.

**How to apply:** Keep original-quality access available and compatibility with external avatars. Ask for separate approval before retroactive processing or cleanup. Measure actual storage traffic before attributing the bill to images.
