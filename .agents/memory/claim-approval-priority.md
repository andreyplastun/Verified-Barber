name: Approval reply priority
description: User-requested claim approvals must precede all other WhatsApp traffic, within the shared cap.

An approved profile claim is a requested service reply, not cold outreach. Send it at the first available channel slot before client messages and specialist nudges. Do not apply the cold invitation or specialist reminder subcaps; retain the visible overall daily cap, protective spacing and sending hours. Brand these messages Rateus, not WHO.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested immediate highest-priority replies after approval, allowing the existing overall message limit. Grouping replies with cold invitations delayed them for days.

**How to apply:** Keep approval replies distinct from cold claim invitations in selection, preemption and subcap accounting. Never claim instant delivery if spacing, time window or the daily cap prevents sending. Historical recovery must skip already-sent replies, owned profiles and invalid phones; replace expired URLs with new tokens instead of reviving expired tokens.
